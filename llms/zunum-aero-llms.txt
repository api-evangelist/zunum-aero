# Zunum Aero

> Zunum Aero is an American aerospace company (founded 2013, Kirkland / Bothell, Washington)
> that developed the ZA10 hybrid-to-electric regional aircraft — a six-to-twelve-seat airplane
> targeting a 700-nautical-mile range — backed by Boeing HorizonX and JetBlue Technology
> Ventures. It laid off nearly all staff in late 2018 and paused operations.
